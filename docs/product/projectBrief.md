# Polar App — Project Brief

---

## Executive Summary

A single-purpose iOS app to monitor heart rate recovery after cardiac ablation. Focused on clarity over features — just heart rate, nothing else.

---

## Problem Statement

I own a Polar watch that does way too many things. The only reason I bought it was to monitor and track my heart rate — that's it.

While the watch is fine for glancing at my current rate, it doesn't easily show me what my heart rate looked like for the entire day. The official Polar app exists, but it's filled with information that isn't relevant to me.

**Context:** I was diagnosed with a-fib and on October 30th had cardiac ablation surgery. I want to track and monitor how my heart rate is doing as I gradually increase activity during recovery.

This isn't a fitness tracker — it's a **recovery companion**.

---

## App Goals

### Core Features

1. **Daily Timeline** — A scrollable view of heart rate throughout the day
2. **Daily Summary** — High / Low / Average, plus percentage of time spent in range
3. **Trends View** — Patterns across days, including:
   - Resting heart rate over time
   - Peak heart rate during activity
   - Time spent in different zones
4. **iOS Widget** — Glanceable current heart rate on the home screen
5. **Threshold Alerts** — Set an upper and lower heart rate; receive haptic notification when out of range

### Deferred for Future

- Different thresholds for resting vs active states
- Distinguishing between brief spikes vs sustained elevation
- Live Activity on lock screen
